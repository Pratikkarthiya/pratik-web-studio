Answers to your queries
How much does a website cost?

Starter is ₹5,999 for a one-page site. Business (up to 5 pages) and Premium (custom design and 3D animations) packages are quoted individually based on your scope and project requirements. Each includes two revision rounds. Domain, hosting, and maintenance are separate.

How fast will my website be ready?

The Starter package is typically completed within 3 to 5 working days after receiving your content and photos. Multi-page Business packages usually take 7 to 10 working days.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, logo if available, list of services, photos, and contact details. We help you organize the content into clean, readable sections.

Will it work on mobile phones?

Yes, 100%. Over 80% of local visitors browse on smartphones, so every website is built mobile-first with high-speed performance.

Can my website be updated later?

Yes. Ongoing maintenance and seasonal content updates can be arranged for ₹999/month, or as needed.
