A few useful answers
How much does a website cost?

Starter is ₹5,999 for a one-page site. Business (up to five pages) and Premium (custom design and 3D animations) packages are quoted individually based on your scope and project requirements. Each includes two revision rounds. Domain, hosting, maintenance, and advanced features are separate. We confirm your final scope and quotation before work begins.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, logo if available, services, photos, and contact details. We can help organise the content into clear website sections.

Will it work on mobile phones?

Yes. Website layouts are designed to adapt to mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.

Can my website be updated later?

Yes. Content updates and ongoing maintenance can be arranged separately, based on your needs.
